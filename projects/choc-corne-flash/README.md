# Choc Corne: left flash, right nice!view

**[Open Draft-Corne](draft-c/README.md)** · [Pin plan](pin-plan.json) · [Current handoff](../../docs/CHATGPT-HANDOFF.md)

Draft C implements the new 42-key Choc geometry, our tested matrix/QSPI, front-mounted nice!nanos with components down, left flash, right nice!view interface and back-side battery solder pads. It is a development draft; see its validation report for routing and check status. Nano/display assembly height and battery retention still need physical review.

The [reference](reference/README.md) is an inferred, unrouted reconstruction of the public Typeractive 3D model, not released electrical source. Drafts A/B and their checks are historical and do not validate Draft C. The Zen case, integrated SoC, RGB and artwork are outside this first build. Working hardware/firmware remains unchanged.
